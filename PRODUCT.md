# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary user is Sassy, the recipient of a personal date invitation. She
experiences the invitation on a phone or tablet, chooses a destination and a
date, and keeps the resulting ticket as a small digital keepsake.

The secondary user is the invitation creator or another couple reusing the
experience by editing `src/config/config.js`.

## Product Purpose

This product turns asking for a date into a short, personal interactive story.
It should make Sassy feel that time and care were invested in something made
specifically for her, then give her a clear, playful way to choose the place and
date and save the confirmed plan.

## Brand Personality

Romantic editorial, dreamy playful, cinematic premium. The voice is intimate,
specific, teasing, and warm without becoming childish or noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI gradients, interchangeable landing-page
templates, dense card grids, game-like confetti, childish sticker collages,
and decorative glass surfaces that do not communicate depth or meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Make the experience feel written and staged for one person.
2. Build emotional anticipation before asking for a decision.
3. Let interaction carry the story, not just decorate it.
4. Keep the romantic details tactile, legible, and restrained.
5. End with a useful keepsake that makes the chosen plan easy to remember.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG basics throughout the experience: readable contrast, semantic
controls, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image alternatives, touch targets
of at least 44px, safe-area support, and a reduced-motion mode that preserves
the journey without disorienting animation.
